Model

2-layer decoder-only byte transformer. 64 dimensions, one causal attention head, 48-byte context, 256-token vocabulary. AdamW + cross-entropy with an in-file autodiff engine. Chat now uses local retrieval and deterministic tools first, then neural next-byte generation.

Corpus: the bundled public-domain text and Megabyte dialogue set, plus your imported local files. IndexedDB stores weights, optimizer state, memories, and training progress on this device.

Experimental small model. Hybrid tools improve reliability, but do not turn this compact model into a frontier LLM. CPU training is local and may be slow.
